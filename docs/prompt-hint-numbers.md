Just as a reminder: Prewritten prompts shall guide LLMs to do the following.

An optional hint for things that are harmless in the display, e.g. "Avoid abbreviations like z. B., u. a., usw." Written out, they read just as well in the chat.
Possibly "write ordinals in words" ("der zweite Weltkrieg"), which is also natural in written German.
Leave digits for quantities, years, prices and formulas, and let the converter handle them.
